Our Story

Powering Homes in Ilorin Since Day One

Aquila Home Tech was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to make quality electronics and home appliances accessible and affordable for every household in Ilorin, Kwara State, and beyond.

We started as a small electrical accessories shop and have grown into a trusted one-stop destination for smart TVs, home theaters, refrigerators, fans, stabilizers and much more. Every product on our shelves is carefully selected for quality, durability, and value.

Our team of experts is passionate about technology and dedicated to helping every customer find the perfect solution for their home. We pride ourselves on honest advice, fair pricing, and exceptional after-sales support.
